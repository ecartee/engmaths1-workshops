    Challenge: Mains Electricity.

    🔗

    8.

    Suppose that mains electricity reaches a factory along three cables, and that the voltages on the cables at time \(t\) seconds are
    \begin{align*} V_1 \amp= A\cos(100\pi t)\\ V_2 \amp= A\cos\left(100\pi t+\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)\\ V_3 \amp= A\cos\left(100\pi t+\frac{4\pi}{3}\right) \end{align*}
    for some constant \(A\text{.}\) Each voltage is a third of a cycle ahead of the one before.
    🔗

    (a)

    Let \(w=e^{2\pi j/3}\text{,}\) which is one of the roots from Exercise 2. Show that \(1+w+w^2=0\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    Each voltage is the real part of a complex number. Use part (a) to show that \(V_1+V_2+V_3=0\) at every time \(t\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    When the three cables feed equal loads, the three currents cancel in the same way. This is why the cables on a pylon come in threes, with no return cable.
